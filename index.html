How to play

Gather every Hearth shard in a realm, then step into the glowing portal. Collect embers: every 10 restores a heart. Burn the Gloom with your Flare. Lanterns save your place.

Collect

Hearth shardGather all of them in a realm to open the portal
EmberEvery 10 restores a heart
Diary pageHidden, often on a secret side platform. Tells the Keeper's story and unlocks an echo at the end of the realm
Power-up orbsShield blocks one hit, Swift runs faster, Moon lowers gravity, Magnet pulls pickups to you
Golden FlameWaits at the end of the Keeper's Trial. Restores hearts and gives a bonus star

Helpers

LanternLights up as a checkpoint. In flood levels it also pushes the Gloom back
Bounce padLaunches you high and refills your jump and dash
Golden ringFace it and press Grapple to zip across big gaps
Keeper's Trial gateStep in to start a short bonus course. Reach its flame to win a prize

Dangers

Gloom wispHurts on touch. Burn it with the Flare or a dash
SpitterA bigger wisp that shoots dark bolts. Dodge or Flare it
Spinning bladeSweeps across the platform. Time your jump over it
Steam geyserGlows, then erupts on a timer. Stay off the glowing ring
Rising GloomIn flood levels the dark climbs the stairs behind you. Keep moving up
WindGusts push you through the air. Steer against them

Ground types

IceSlippery. You keep sliding after you let go
Moving platformSlides back and forth and carries you with it
Crumbling stoneShakes and falls away shortly after you land
Fading platformAppears and disappears on a timer. Wait for it
Light bridgeOnly solid while lit. Use the Flare nearby to wake it for a few seconds
